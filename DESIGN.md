## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-perfumery editorial poise, artisanal provenance, and meditative quiet luxury. Evoking the ambiance of a private Parisian or Grasse flacon atelier, the interface prioritizes expansive negative space, tactile material palettes, and restrained typographic balance.

The visual style merges **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Modernism**:
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Warm alabaster, porous limestone, raw travertine, and obsidian charcoal replace harsh digital whites and generic grays.
- **Poetic Rhythm:** Spacious compositions prioritize visual breathing room, allowing bottle geometry, flacon translucency, and olfactory accords to command attention.
- **Deliberate Restraint:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke haute horlogerie and editorial print publications, counterbalanced by monospaced, hyper-tracked geometric metadata.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from natural perfumery components, mineral extraction stones, and classical parchment:
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F2EB`):** A warm, unbleached linen tone serving as the default foundation across viewport containers.
- **Primary Ink (`#181715`):** Deep obsidian charcoal with warm undertones; prevents the clinical glare of synthetic pure black while delivering uncompromising legibility.
- **Secondary & Tertiary Earth Accents (`#CEB39E`, `#917055`):** Distilled sand, ambrette seed, and aged cedar tones used for delicate badges, olfactory pyramid indicators, and secondary focus states.
- **Structural Borders (`#E2DDD5`, `#D8D2C7`):** Razor-thin mineral stone lines providing crisp geometric alignment without breaking atmospheric immersion.

## Typography

The typography stages an ongoing dialogue between classical literature and modern industrial design:
- **Serif Display (`Playfair Display`):** Utilized for editorial brand expressions, product naming conventions, and narrative headings. Italic styling is reserved selectively for evocative descriptors, olfactive attributes, and poetic subheadings (e.g., *Extrait de Parfum*, *The Art of Quiet Luxury*).
- **Body & Meta (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and functional. All operational indicators, indexing tags (e.g., `01 // SOURCING`), buttons, and technical specifications must be rendered in tracked, uppercase, or calibrated regular weights to keep the interface legible and grounded.

## Layout & Spacing

The architectural layout relies on a strict 12-column grid with generous sectional pauses:
- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed at 20px on mobile viewports, escalating to a deliberate 64px on wide screens to maintain cinematic framing.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Major editorial transitions implement `space-2xl` (80px) and `space-3xl` (128px) breaks to enforce unhurried, contemplative exploration.
- **Centering Strategy:** Hero narratives and brand headers occupy symmetric center-locked blocks, while product galleries, accordions, and atelier notes settle into asymmetric 4-column and 8-column arrangements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via delicate tonal shifts and physical surface materiality rather than layered drop shadows:
- **Hairline Framing:** Surface separation is achieved with subtle 1px border lines in `border-hairline` (`#E2DDD5`), eliminating dark, murky drop shadows.
- **Diffused Alabaster Lift:** When elevation is functionally mandatory (such as floating bag drawers or modal viewports), apply a single, highly diffused ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(24, 23, 21, 0.06)`.
- **Atmospheric Layering:** Layer `#FFFFFF` cards over the base `#F5F2EB` canvas to gently lift product photography from its environment without creating synthetic elevation tiers.

## Shapes

The design system employs an intentional pairing of architectural sharpness and soft capsule accents:
- **Containers & Framing (`0px`):** Product media cards, accordion panels, inputs, and section dividers maintain pristine 90-degree right angles, evoking tailored stationery and perfume boxes.
- **Capsule Exceptions (`Pill`):** Action-oriented interactive elements (primary button capsules, status pills, and filter chips) use full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an ergonomic, tactile contrast against the crisp rectangular framework.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Capsule:** Solid obsidian black (`#151515`) fill, crisp off-white (`#FAF8F5`) text, uppercase `label-caps`, padded with 16px vertical by 36px horizontal, `border-radius: 9999px`. Subtle scale on active tap (0.98).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px border (`#181715`), obsidian black text. On hover, transitions to warm stone parchment background (`#F8F6F1`).
- **Text Link CTA:** Understated inline text in `label-caps` followed by a delicate horizontal arrow (`→`) or glyph with a 1px baseline underline offset by 4px.

### Navigation Header
- **Layout:** Three-part balance with brand wordmark centered in light serif capitals (`GAAYA`).
- **Left Column:** Minimal category links (`Collection`, `Craftsmanship`, `Journal`) in `label-caps` with 24px spacing.
- **Right Column:** Utility actions (`Search`, `Bag (0)`) rendered in light sans-serif with hairline divider accents.

### Product Collection Cards
- **Structure:** Tall portrait frame (3:4 or 4:5 aspect ratio) featuring pedestal or flacon photography set against `#FAF8F5`.
- **Card Content:** Placed directly beneath the photograph without heavy bounding containers. Includes index numbering (`01 // PARFUM`), title in `headline-sm`, olfactory notes in `body-sm` (`text-secondary`), and monetary value in clean `label-meta`.
- **Badge Indicators:** Minimal sand-tone pills (`#CEB39E` background with `#181715` text) floating subtly in the top-left quadrant of imagery.

### Form Inputs & Enquiry Fields
- **Input Baseline:** Understated bottom-border-only style (1px `#D8D2C7`) or crisp rectangular boxes in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E2DDD5` stroke.
- **Typography:** Floating placeholder labels using `label-meta` in `#76726A`.
- **Focus State:** Stroke smoothly transitions to obsidian ink (`#181715`) with zero glow or fuzzy outline.

### Volumetric Accordion
- **Structure:** Stacked horizontal strips separated by 1px `#E2DDD5` hairline dividers.
- **Trigger:** Left-aligned step indicator (`01`, `02`) alongside `headline-sm` title; right-aligned delicate `+` / `−` icon.
- **Content Panel:** Expands downward revealing fragrance notes (Top, Heart, Base) arranged in a balanced 3-column micro-grid.
