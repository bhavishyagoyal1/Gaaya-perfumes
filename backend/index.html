Who We Are

India's Emerging
Fragrance Creator

Gaaya Perfumes Private Limited is a proudly Indian fragrance manufacturer, incorporated in January 2024 and registered under RoC-Jaipur. Located at the RIICO Industrial Area in Khushkhera, Tapukara, Alwar, Rajasthan — we are at the heart of India's growing chemical and cosmetic industry.

We specialize in the manufacture of perfumes, attars, and cosmetic chemical products — crafting bespoke fragrances for businesses across India. Our state-of-the-art facility is built to meet the highest standards of quality and precision.
